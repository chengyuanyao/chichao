# Impact foley

Source: Kenney, Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019-12-19).
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

License: CC0; original license retained in KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.

- metal-impact.ogg: impactMetal_heavy_000.ogg (unaltered)
- rubble-impact.ogg: impactMining_000.ogg (unaltered)
- crystal-impact.ogg: impactGlass_heavy_000.ogg (unaltered)

These are impact/foley layers, not recordings of actual weapons. They are mixed
with locally synthesized transients and low-frequency body during audio preload.
Downloads/decoding never occur on the weapon event path. Failure preserves the
bounded synthesized fallback. No extra live audio voices are added by layering.
